# Reference Library and Citation Roles

## Purpose

This registry identifies the exact papers supplied for the preprint work, gives a citation-ready record, and states how each source may support the Slay-ILS-Designer argument. The source PDFs are not committed to this repository. Their checksums identify the reviewed copies without redistributing third-party files.

## EDIKB Citation Rule

Use R7 for claims derived from the `paper1_inline_behaviour` source family. Use R8 for `paper2_east_behaviour`, `paper2_easb_behaviour`, and `paper2_assembly_mass_branch_behaviour`. A paper-level citation establishes provenance, but it does not by itself validate every numeric row. Before publication, each quantitative EDIKB claim must also identify its source table, figure, case, parameter range, response location, and stated limitation.

The two papers are related and should be cited together when a statement spans component-level and assembly-level behavior. Do not transfer an R7 isolated-component trend to an R8 assembly without an explicit interaction argument or supporting case, and do not generalize either paper outside its analyzed S-lay configuration and parameter ranges.

## Remaining Literature Gaps

The supplied set closes the immediate P-map, OAM, human-in-the-loop LLM/KG design, APF, and EDIKB source gaps. A citation-ready manuscript still needs original FBS design-theory sources, engineering knowledge-graph literature, foundational RAG/tool-using-agent references, active-learning/surrogate-modeling references, and applicable subsea design-code sources.
